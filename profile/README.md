# 🚀 Appliman

**Building modern SaaS solutions with .NET, Cloud & AI**

Appliman is a software company focused on designing and building **scalable web applications** for businesses and associations.  
We specialize in **.NET ecosystems, Blazor, Aspire cloud-native architectures, and SaaS products**.

Founded in 2024, Appliman aims to help organizations accelerate their digital transformation by delivering:

- Cloud-native SaaS platforms
- Scalable and maintainable architectures
- Modern developer tooling & open-source solutions

Our mission is simple:  
👉 **Turn business ideas into robust, production-ready software**
